# XVault workbench

Existing desktop tokens are the visual authority: matte Graphite surfaces, warm brass accent, Hanken Grotesk interface type, Newsreader post text, clear separators and restrained corner radii. Media carries color; controls carry hierarchy and state.

Remote adapts that workbench into a thumb-operated archive: a compact connection header, persistent navigation, readable search/filters, a media contact sheet, and protected post/note sheets. Phone text and touch targets stay readable at 320px. No marketing labels or ornamental card scaffolds.

X integration uses the same geometry and icon stroke in a compact utility language, with colors derived from X's light/dark background. It is recognizably XVault and does not cover or imitate X controls.

Motion is spatial and interruptible: navigation moves along its order, media changes along the browsing direction, sheets enter from their edge, and state indicators explain success/error. Native playback gestures and text editing retain ownership. Global motion constraints suppress movement.

Remote's Browse sheet puts month navigation above a flat searchable saved-view list. Its surface stays opaque throughout spatial entry, including Paper. Date constraints are explicit and removable; changed desktop results use a quiet refresh banner. Installation is offered after pairing without blocking Library, and dismissal is remembered. The optional privacy cover hides immediately with no animation and requires an explicit reveal; it never presents itself as authentication.

Remote downloads name the destination: Save to PC queues captured media, Save to phone streams a local file. Gallery uses a compact monitor-arrow action; sheets put download progress and Stop above notes. Success/pressed motion belongs to the glyph so the 44px hit area never shrinks. Mobile cards give authors a full row, and very narrow layouts switch to one column. Viewer contact frames use small previews and icon fallbacks; missing-file retry preserves position/focus. Independent view scroll and measured virtual spacers preserve browsing continuity.
